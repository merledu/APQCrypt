3 · System condition and decision table
Before choosing a lock, APQCrypt checks how busy the computer is. This page shows the live reading and the table the engine uses.
Why this matters: small IoT devices have little spare power. When the computer is busy (CPU or memory over the limit), the engine picks cheaper locks. But the most sensitive traffic (HIGH) always keeps its quantum-safe key exchange. Only the cheap parts adapt. The limits (CPU 50%, memory 70%) are our own design values. They should be replaced by numbers measured on the RISC-V board.
