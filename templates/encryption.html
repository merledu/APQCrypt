4 · Encryption engine
Each conversation gets one key exchange, then all its packets are locked with the chosen cipher.
How it works, step by step:
1) Key exchange (once per conversation): normal X25519, or X25519 together with ML-KEM (quantum-safe) for sensitive traffic. Using both is called hybrid.
2) Key making: the shared secrets are mixed with HKDF to produce the real key.
3) Locking: every packet's data is encrypted with AES-GCM or ChaCha20-Poly1305. Both also detect tampering.
4) Self-checks: we decrypt a sample to prove it works, and flip one bit to prove tampering is caught. Limit: in this prototype both "sides" of the exchange run in the same program.
