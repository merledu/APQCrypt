6 · Debugging interface
Look inside every processing step: what went in, what came out, which settings were used, and what happened to one conversation from start to finish.
What you can do here:
1) Run to a step and stop, or re-run from a step using the files the earlier steps already saved.
2) Inspect a step: its settings, its result numbers, a sample of its output file, and its log lines.
3) Trace one conversation through all 8 stages and see the value at each stage.
4) Watch the live log while a run is going, and download a debug bundle (settings, log, and all result files) for a bug report.
