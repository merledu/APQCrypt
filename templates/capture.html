1 · Capture and Features
We create a small test network, record the traffic that flows through it, and turn every recorded packet into a row of facts.
In simple words: a packet is one small piece of data on the network. We record about 1000 of them, then write down for each one: who sent it, who received it, which door (port), how big it was, the TCP flags, a conversation ID, and the first bytes of its data. Those first bytes let the next stage "peek" at plain-text clues like a website name. Nothing is decrypted: we only read what the network protocols send in the open.
