Sensitivity scoring

Two independent assessments of each flow are combined into one sensitivity level.

How the score is calculated

Confidentiality score (rules). What kind of data is this? The destination port and protocol give a base score (for example SSH or a banking site scores high, clock synchronisation scores low). Clear-text protocol fields refine it: the TLS server name, the DNS query type, the HTTP method and Authorization header, and the SSH banner.

Behaviour risk (Random Forest). Does the flow behave like an attack? A Random Forest trained on CIC-IDS2017 flow features returns LOW, MEDIUM or HIGH.

Final score = max(confidentiality score, risk score)
0.70 or above: HIGH   0.40 or above: MEDIUM   below 0.40: LOW

The maximum is used so that a strong signal is never diluted by averaging. One decision is made per flow, from the highest score in its first 10 packets. When the classifier is uncertain, protection is raised, never lowered.
