2 · Sensitivity scoring
Two independent opinions about every conversation are combined into one sensitivity level.
Opinion 1: Confidentiality (rules). What kind of data is this? We use the port number and protocol (e.g. 22 = remote login, 123 = clock sync) and we peek at plain-text clues: a bank-like website name, an e-mail DNS question, an HTTP login header, an SSH greeting.
Opinion 2: Behaviour risk (AI). Does the traffic behave like an attack? A Random Forest trained on the CIC-IDS2017 attack data gives LOW, MEDIUM or HIGH.
Final score = MAX( Confidentiality score , Risk score )
≥ 0.70 → HIGH   ≥ 0.40 → MEDIUM   below → LOW
We take the maximum so a strong signal is never watered down by an average. One decision is made per conversation: the highest score in its first 10 packets. If the AI is unsure, protection is raised, never lowered.
